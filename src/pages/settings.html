Tor

A Tor window sends all its traffic through the Tor network, can open .onion sites, keeps nothing after you close it, blocks WebRTC IP leaks, and disables cloud AI and search suggestions. It is not as resistant to fingerprinting as the official Tor Browser — for high-risk use, use Tor Browser.

AI assistant

Free options: Ollama or LM Studio run on your PC (private, no key). OpenRouter “:free” models, Groq and Gemini have free tiers. Keys are encrypted with Windows' secure storage and never shared with websites.

Extensions

Load unpacked Chrome extensions (a folder with manifest.json). Support is partial: content scripts and many background features work, but toolbar pop-ups and some Chrome APIs don't.
